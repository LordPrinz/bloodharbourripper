# 🩸 The Bloodharbour Ripper

> *"The sea takes everyone, eventually. I just help it along."*

Step into the boots of Bilgewater's most feared killer. **The Bloodharbour Ripper** brings Pyke's
drowned arsenal to Minecraft: a family of harpoons you throw, recall and upgrade, a brutal
execution that drags your victims to the depths, and a phantom dash that leaves enemies blind and
frozen in your wake.

## 🦴 The Bone Skewers

All three skewers work like swords in close combat. **Hold right click** to charge and **release
to throw** — the harpoon flies like a loyal trident, hooks its target and **pulls it towards you**,
then returns to your hand. While it is in flight, **right click again to recall it** instantly.

Shields are no protection: a skewer that hits a raised shield knocks it out of use for 5 seconds.

| Tier | Melee | Thrown | On hit |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Bone Skewer Skeleton** | 7 | 7 | Pull |
| **Bone Skewer Raw** | 9 | 8 | Pull, Slowness II (2 s) |
| **Bone Skewer** | 15 | 15 | Strong pull, Slowness II (4 s), Wither (8 s) + both abilities |

Skewers accept sword enchantments (Sharpness, Smite, Looting, Unbreaking, Mending…), which also
apply to the throw.

## 💀 Death From Below — `Shift` + `C`

*Bone Skewer in your main hand.*

While you hold the Bone Skewer, every enemy within **12 blocks** that drops **below 33% health**
is outlined — only you can see it. Press the key and the closest one is **marked**: a glowing sigil
opens beneath it, and a moment later the sea claims it.

- The target is **executed**, whatever its remaining health.
- It drops its **loot and experience a second time**.
- You are **pulled to its position** in a burst of bubbles and spray.
- Allies, tamed pets of you and your team, and creative/spectator players are never valid targets.
- Cooldown: **5 seconds**.

## 👻 Phantom Undertow — `Shift` + `X`

*Bone Skewer in your main hand.*

Lunge forward in a burst of speed. A spectral copy of you — wearing your skin — lingers behind,
then **rushes back to you**, and every enemy on its path takes **8 damage** and is **blinded,
frozen and weakened for 3 seconds**.

- Cooldown: **5 seconds**.
- Both key bindings can be changed in *Options → Controls* under **Blood Harbour Ripper**.

## 🐟 Jaull Fish Vial

A mysterious vial found only in **Ancient City chests** (33% chance per chest). It is the smithing
template that awakens the Bone Skewer — you will need one for each upgrade.

## ⚒️ Crafting & progression

**1. Bone Skewer Skeleton** — *Crafting Table*

```
 ·  P  P        P = Prismarine Shard
 ·  N  P        N = Nautilus Shell
 B  ·  ·        B = Bone
```

**2. Bone Skewer Raw** — *Smithing Table*

`Jaull Fish Vial` + `Bone Skewer Skeleton` + `Echo Shard`

**3. Bone Skewer** — *Smithing Table*

`Jaull Fish Vial` + `Bone Skewer Raw` + `Heart of the Sea`

Smithing upgrades **keep enchantments, custom names and durability**. Recipes unlock in the recipe
book as soon as you pick up an ingredient. Obtain the final Bone Skewer to earn the challenge
advancement **"Sink them all!"**.

## ❓ FAQ

**Which file do I download?** Pick the file matching your Minecraft version and loader —
`forge`, `neoforge` or `fabric` is in the file name.

**Can I rebind the abilities?** Yes, in *Options → Controls → Blood Harbour Ripper*.

**Found a bug?** Please report it in the comments with your Minecraft version, loader and the log.
